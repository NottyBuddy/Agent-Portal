# Part B — Product

## 7. uPVC Ko 90 Seconds Mein Samjhein
uPVC ka full form unplasticised polyvinyl chloride hai. Ye ordinary soft PVC jaisa nahi; ek rigid engineered material hai jo window aur door profiles mein use hota hai.

uPVC rust nahi karta, rot nahi hota aur routine painting ki zaroorat nahi hoti. Proper configuration aur installation ke saath ye heat, noise, dust aur rain ke against sealing mein madad kar sakta hai.

Profile ke andar hollow chambers design ka hissa hote hain. Ye air chambers insulation mein madad karte hain. Frame ki strength ke liye galvanised steel reinforcement use ho sakta hai. Exact chamber count, thickness ya rating agent quote na kare.

### 7.1 Common customer questions
**“Ye plastic hai, tootega ya bend hoga?”**
“Sir, uPVC ordinary soft plastic nahi hai. Ye rigid engineered profile hai aur frame ke andar galvanised steel reinforcement bhi hota hai. Exact configuration hamari technical team confirm karegi.”

**“Colour yellow ho jayega?”**
“Sir, profile aur finish ke liye approved colour/warranty options available hain. Aap jo colour choose karenge, uske exact warranty terms Sales team written form mein confirm karegi.”

**“Garmi mein expand ya crack hoga?”**
“Installation ke dauran frame ke liye required fitting gap aur sealing process follow hota hai. Aapke site ke hisaab se exact technical detail hamari team confirm karegi.”

**“Kitne saal chalega?”**
“Durability selected profile, hardware, usage aur maintenance par depend karti hai. Approved warranty terms main aapko bata sakta hoon; exact technical life claim ke liye Technical team se confirm karenge.”

**“Painting/maintenance chahiye?”**
“Generally regular painting ya polishing ki zaroorat nahi hoti. Routine cleaning kaafi hoti hai.”

Chamber count, wall thickness, wind load, thermal rating ya numerical performance ka jawab na dein.

**Inbound:** “Good morning, IndiFrame se [Name] bol raha/rahi hoon. Main aapki kya help kar sakta/sakti hoon?” Customer ko bolne dein. Phir permission lein: “Sahi team se connect karne ke liye site ke baare mein do chhoti details le sakta/sakti hoon?”

**Outbound digital/event lead:** “Good morning Sir, main [Name], IndiFrame se bol raha/rahi hoon. Aapne apni site ke windows ke liye enquiry ki thi. Kya abhi do minute baat karna convenient hai?” Busy ho toh exact callback slot poochein.

**Spotted lead:** “Hamari team ne [location] par aapki site visit ki thi. Humein pata chala construction chal raha hai. Main check karna chahta/chahti tha ki windows final ki hain ya abhi requirement open hai?” CRM notes pehle padhein.

**Nurture/follow-up:** Pichhli discussion aur follow-up ka reason batayein—construction stage, requested callback, quotation ya Experience Centre update.

**“Number kahan se mila?”** Source honestly batayein. Customer baat nahi karna chahe toh respect karein aur contact preference record karein.

## 16. Discovery — Chhe Zaroori Sawal
Har qualification call mein ye six points conversationally capture karein:
1. **Residential ya commercial:** “Ye aapke ghar ke liye hai Sir ya commercial project?”
2. **Site location:** “Site kis area mein hai? PIN code bhi share kar sakte hain?”
3. **Customer ka role:** “Aap khud construction karwa rahe hain ya architect/designer ke through project manage ho raha hai?”
4. **Construction stage:** “Abhi construction kis stage par hai? Brickwork complete hai ya plastering start hui?”
5. **Approx quantity:** “Roughly kitni windows aur doors chahiye?”
6. **Readiness:** “Openings par plastering complete ho gayi hai?”

### 16.1 Answers ke alawa kya sunna hai
- Deadline: housewarming, possession, wedding, monsoon—CRM mein note aur Sales ko flag.
- Pain point: road noise, dust, heat, leakage—customer ke exact words note karein.
- Decision maker: spouse, architect, contractor, parent—naam/contact capture karein.
- Competitor involved ho toh neutral note karein; react ya criticise na karein.

Quantity clear na ho toh rough estimate ya bedroom count se context lein. Price calculation na karein.

### 16.2 Qualification close
**Site ready:** “Main aapki free site consultation arrange karwata hoon. Team requirement samjhegi, openings assess karegi aur exact quotation degi. Kya [day/time] convenient hai?”
**Site ready nahi:** “Koi baat nahi Sir. Sahi construction stage par follow-up karenge. Main [month] ke aas-paas callback note kar leta hoon. Aap chahein toh Experience Centre visit kar sakte hain.”
**Pehle dekhna chahte hain:** Experience Centre ka current verified address WhatsApp par share karein aur preferred visit date/time note karein.

“Hum wapas call karenge” jaise vague close na karein. Next step, owner aur timeframe clear karein.

## 18. Words We Use / Words We Avoid
- “Showroom” ke bajay **Experience Centre**.

- “Guarantee” ke bajay **Warranty**.
- “Replace karenge” ke bajay **Fix/theek karenge**.
- “Cheap/cheaper” ke bajay **Value** ya **better value**.
- “Material” kehne ke bajay customer context mein **windows and doors**.
- “I think/maybe/probably” ke bajay **Main confirm karke batata hoon**.
- “Hum best hain” na kahein; facts aur process explain karein.
- Paise ke liye “sirf/chhota amount” jaise judgement words na use karein.

## 19. Glossary
- **Profile:** uPVC ka frame section, jisse window banti hai.
- **Frame:** Wall mein fix hone wala outer part.
- **Shutter/Sash:** Move hone wala panel.
- **Opening:** Wall ka woh space jahan window/door fit hota hai.
- **Plastering:** Wall finishing; site readiness ka important marker.
- **Typology:** Window/door ka type.
- **Glazing:** Glass aur uski fitting.
- **DGU:** Double Glazed Unit; do panes aur sealed air gap.
- **Toughened glass:** Heat-treated safety glass.
- **Laminate:** Profile par decorative finish.
- **Hardware:** Handles, locks, hinges, rollers.
- **Gasket:** Glass/frame ke beech rubber seal.
- **Reinforcement:** Profile ke andar galvanised steel.
- **Sq ft:** Square foot; quotation ka area unit. Agent dimensions se price calculate na kare.
- **BOQ:** Bill of Quantities; quotation ka itemised breakdown.
- **Experience Centre:** Product experience location.
- **Spotting:** Field team ka construction site identify karna.
- **B2F:** Fabricator-facing business route; customer lead ko is route mein sirf approved process se transfer karein.
- **SR:** Service Request; post-installation issue record.
