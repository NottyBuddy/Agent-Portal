Product Information
Is uPVC strong?
It's plastic.
uPVC is a rigid, engineered plastic known as unplasticized polyvinyl chloride—completely different from ordinary soft PVC. It is
exceptionally hard, dimensionally stable, and built to maintain its structural shape for decades.

 Will it yellow or crack in the heat, and how long does it last?
Because it is engineered with unplasticized polyvinyl chloride, it resists heat warping and discoloration. It is structurally designed to last
for decades without cracking or turning yellow.

What colors do you have?
We offer eight versatile color and laminate options, including a 7-year dedicated color warranty on solid grey and solid black profiles.

Do you provide mosquito mesh, grills, or child locks?
Yes, we offer all these integrated features to suit your safety and comfort needs.

What is DGU?
DGU stands for Double Glazed Unit. It features two glass panes separated by a sealed air gap, which dramatically reduces outdoor noise
and heat—making it an ideal choice for road-facing rooms.

We are planning to launch aluminum windows around October. Our sales team can confirm the current availability and details.

Do you make doors?
Yes, alongside our windows, we offer high-quality uPVC doors. We will also be introducing wooden flush doors, ABS, and WPC doors later
this year.

Do you do railings?
Not just yet, but railings will be added to our product line later this year.

railings coming soon after, our goal is to be your single trusted brand for all openings.

Window & Door Styles Quick Guide

Sliding Windows/Doors: Shutters glide smoothly sideways on tracks; the top choice for modern Indian homes and balconies.
Casement Windows/Doors: Hinged to open outward or inward like a traditional door, offering a tighter acoustic and weather seal.
Fixed Windows: Non-opening glass panels designed to maximize natural light and outdoor views.
Tilt & Turn: Dual-action windows that tilt inward from the top for secure ventilation or swing fully open.
Sliding Folding: Panels that fold completely away to open up entire wall spans, perfect for expansive balconies.
Bay & Combination: Projecting multi-section windows or multi-panel modular setups built for custom architectural facades.\
Lift & Slide Doors: Heavy-duty sliding mechanisms engineered for extra-large openings while remaining light and smooth to operate.

Glossary
             Term                                           What it means

            Profile       The uPVC frame section itself, before it becomes a window. Green Fortune's product.

            Frame                              The fixed outer part, anchored to the wall.

       Shutter / sash                                     The part that moves.

     Opening / aperture                       The hole in the wall where the window goes.

           Plastering                            Wall finishing. Our readiness marker.

           Typology                            The type of window or door. Drives price.

            Glazing                                     The glass, and fitting it.

             DGU                  Double Glazed Unit. Two panes, sealed air gap. Cuts noise and heat.

      Toughened glass                    Heat-treated safety glass; breaks into blunt granules.

           Laminate                Decorative finish on the profile. Our five wood and colour options.

           Hardware                                  Handles, locks, hinges, rollers.

            Gasket               The rubber seal between glass and frame. Keeps water and noise out.

       Reinforcement              Galvanised steel inside the profile. Where the strength comes from.

             Sq ft                     How windows are priced. Width × height of the opening.

             BOQ                    Bill of Quantities. The itemised breakdown behind a quotation.

      Experience centre                Kukatpally, Hyderabad. Jubilee Hills from mid-September.

           Spotting                        Our field team identifying live construction sites.

              B2F                The fabricator-facing team under Green Fortune. Where C7 leads go.

              SR                                Service Request. Post-installation issues.
