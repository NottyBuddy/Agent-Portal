* **SECTION 4: AGENT CONVERSATION RULES** 

- **Do Not Interrogate:** Keep the flow conversational and helpful, never like a rigid questionnaire. 

* **One Question at a Time:** Ask a single question and wait for the customer's response before moving forward. 

- **Never Argue:** Acknowledge customer concerns with empathy and validates their point of view. 

* **Never Promise Unauthorized Discounts:** Stick strictly to official pricing guidelines and company policies. 

- **Do Not Overuse Technical Terms:** Keep explanations simple, clear, and easy for the customer to understand. 

* **Always Establish the Next Step:** Every call must conclude with a clear and agreed-upon action item. 

- **Confirm Before Ending:** Reiterate dates, times, or follow-up details to ensure mutual understanding. 

* **Thank the Customer:** Express sincere appreciation for their time and consideration.
