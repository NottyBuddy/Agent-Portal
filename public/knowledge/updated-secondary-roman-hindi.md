## 1. Calling Standards & Agent Conduct
- Har call ko helpful consultation ki tarah handle karein, rigid questionnaire ki tarah nahi.
- Ek baar mein ek hi sawal poochhein; customer ko jawab dene ka samay dein.
- Dhyan se sunein, concern acknowledge karein aur poori conversation supportive rakhein.
- Simple language use karein; unnecessary technical terms avoid karein.
- Competitors ya local fabricators ko criticize na karein. IndiFrame ke approved process aur value par focus karein.
- Unauthorized discount, exact delivery date, structural approval, wind-load calculation, mm-level specification, chamber count ya bina technical validation ke koi commitment na dein.
- Har connected call ko clear aur mutually agreed next action ke saath close karein.
- Call end karne se pehle requirement, concerned team, action, date aur time reconfirm karein.
- Installation planning granite/marble sill work complete hone ke baad karna best hai. Site condition aur technical team ki confirmation ke bina installation promise na karein.

## 2. Six Mandatory Discovery Questions
Lead stage assign karne se pehle, jahan conversation allow kare, ye chhe questions complete karein:
1. **Project Type:** “Sir, ye project aapke personal residential home ke liye hai ya commercial property ke liye?”
2. **Site Location:** “Aapki construction site kis city aur exact area/locality mein hai?”
3. **Stakeholder Role:** “Aap construction khud manage kar rahe hain ya architect/interior designer ke saath kaam kar rahe hain?”
4. **Construction Stage:** “Site abhi kis stage par hai—foundation, brickwork, plastering ya finishing?”
5. **Quantity Estimate:** “Approximate kitne window aur door openings cover karne hain?”
6. **Aperture Readiness:** “Kya sabhi window openings ke andar aur bahar ki plastering complete ho chuki hai?”

Capture: Residential/Commercial/Villa/Apartment; area + PIN code and serviceability; Self/Architect/Civil Contractor/Designer; Foundation/Brickwork/Plastering/Interiors; approximate window-door count; Ready/In progress.

## 3. Lead Stage, Routing & CRM Rules
- **C0 — New Unqualified:** Lead assign hote hi first call attempt karein.
- **C1 — Future Potential:** Foundation, slab, brickwork ya plastering incomplete hai. Expected readiness date ke hisaab se CRM follow-up set karein.
- **C2 — Active / Qualified:** Plastering complete aur openings audit ke liye ready hain. Daily Leads WhatsApp Group mein customer contact details post karein, designated Regional Sales Owner ko tag karein aur free site consultation/site audit ya Experience Centre visit offer karein.
- **C7 — Outside Direct Coverage:** Location direct installation coverage se bahar ho to approved Green Fortune team/local routing verify karein. CRM mein C7 / ROUTE TO B2F record karein. Full IndiFrame direct installation coverage ka promise na karein.
- **C4 — Won:** Payment complete/manufacturing start hone par order processing/manufacturing handover initiate karein aur confirmation customer ko share karein.
- **C6 — Lost:** Specific loss reason CRM mein record karein, status Lost karein aur applicable ho to long-term nurture/feedback follow-up schedule karein.
- **Pricing:** uPVC ke liye approved starting reference sirf “₹450 per sq. ft. onwards + applicable GST” hai. Custom calculation, single-window estimate ya unauthorized discount nahi dena. Aluminium pricing, specification aur timeline Sales ko route karein.
- **Warranty:** Source reference mein 15-year profile, 5-year hardware aur 7-year colour warranty diya hai. Customer ko current written warranty/terms confirm karne ke liye Sales se verify karayein; exclusions ya unsupported terms invent na karein.
- **Retry cadence:** Maximum 15 attempts / 9 calendar days, varied time slots, exact disposition after each attempt. Day 1: 3; Days 2–4: 2 per day; remaining 6 attempts Days 5–9 mein alag-alag time slots par complete karein.
