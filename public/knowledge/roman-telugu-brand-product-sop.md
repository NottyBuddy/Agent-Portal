## 7. uPVC Ni 90 Seconds Lo Explain Cheyyadam
uPVC ante unplasticised polyvinyl chloride. Idi ordinary soft PVC laantidi kaadu; window mariyu door profiles kosam use chese rigid engineered material.

uPVC rust avvadu, rot avvadu, regular painting avasaram undadu. Correct configuration mariyu installation tho heat, noise, dust mariyu rain against sealing ki help chestundi.

Profile lopala hollow chambers design lo part. Ee air chambers insulation ki help chestayi. Frame strength kosam galvanised steel reinforcement undachu. Exact chamber count, thickness leda rating agent cheppakudadhu.
