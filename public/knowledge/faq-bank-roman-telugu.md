## 7. Window & Door Styles — Quick Guide
- **Sliding Windows/Doors:** Shutters tracks meeda sideways slide avuthayi. Modern homes, balconies lo commonly use chestaru.
- **Casement Windows/Doors:** Hinges meeda inward/outward open avuthayi. Sealing/performance exact system/configuration batti depend avuthundi.
- **Fixed Windows:** Open avvani glass panels; natural light, outside view kosam.
- **Tilt & Turn:** Dual-action system; top nunchi tilt ventilation kosam leda full swing-open mode.
- **Sliding Folding / Slide & Fold:** Panels fold ayi opening ki wide access istayi. Panel count/configuration catalogue mariyu technical approval batti confirm cheyyandi.
- **Bay & Combination:** Project ayye multi-section windows leda multiple panels modular combinations.
- **Lift & Slide Doors:** Large openings kosam engineered sliding mechanism; size/suitability Technical team confirm chestundi.

## 11. Six Mandatory Discovery Questions — English Source Fields
1. Project Type: “Is this project for your personal residential home or a commercial property?” Capture Residential / Commercial / Villa / Apartment.
2. Site Location: “Which specific city and area/locality is your construction site located in?” Capture area + PIN code; verify serviceability.
3. Stakeholder Role: “Are you managing the build directly, or working with an architect or interior designer?” Capture Self / Architect / Civil Contractor / Designer.
4. Construction Stage: “What stage is the site currently in—Foundation, Brickwork, Plastering, or Finishing?” Capture Foundation / Brickwork / Plastering / Interiors.
5. Quantity Estimate: “Approximately how many window and door apertures/openings do you need to cover?” Capture estimated window/door count.
6. Aperture Readiness: “Has internal and external plastering been completed around all window openings?” Capture Yes (ready) / No (in progress).
