## 1. Agent Conversation Principles

- Har call ko ek helpful consultation ki tarah handle karein, na ki ek fixed questionnaire ki tarah.
- Ek baar mein sirf ek sawaal poochhein aur agla sawaal poochhne se pehle customer ko jawab dene dein.
- Dhyan se sunein, customer ki concerns ko acknowledge karein aur poori conversation mein supportive tone rakhein.
- Explanations simple rakhein aur unnecessary technical terms ka use na karein.
- Competitors ya local fabricators ki burai na karein; conversation ko IndiFrame ki value ki taraf le jaayein.
- Unauthorized discounts, exact delivery dates, structural approvals, wind-load calculations, mm-level specifications, chamber counts, ya kisi bhi aise technical detail ka promise na karein jiske liye technical validation zaroori ho.
- Har connected call ka end ek clear aur mutually agreed next action ke saath hona chahiye.
- Call end karne se pehle, jahan applicable ho, customer ki requirement aur agreed action, team, date aur time ko dobara confirm karein.
- Installation ki planning granite ya marble sill ka kaam complete hone ke baad karna behtar hota hai. Isse proper fitting mein madad milti hai aur site damage ka risk kam hota hai.

## 2. Mandatory Discovery Questions

Conversation allow kare to lead stage assign karne se pehle ye chhe questions complete karein.

**Project Type:** Kya yeh project aapke personal residential home ke liye hai ya commercial property ke liye?

**Site Location:** Aapki construction site kis specific city aur area/locality mein located hai?

**Stakeholder Role:** Kya aap khud construction manage kar rahe hain, ya aapke saath architect ya interior designer bhi involved hain?

**Construction Stage:** Abhi site kis stage par hai — Foundation, Brickwork, Plastering, ya Finishing?

**Quantity Estimate:** Aapko approximately kitne window aur door apertures/openings cover karne hain?

**Aperture Readiness:** Kya sabhi window openings ke around internal aur external plastering complete ho chuki hai?

## 3. Lead Stage & Routing Rules

**C0 – New Unqualified Lead:** Lead pehli baar system mein aayi hai. Assignment milte hi first call initiate karein.

**C1 – Future Potential Lead:** Foundation, slab, brickwork, ya plastering abhi complete nahi hui hai. Expected readiness date ke basis par follow-up schedule karein.

**C2 – Active / Qualified Lead:** Plastering complete hai aur apertures audit ke liye ready hain. Customer ke contact details Daily Leads WhatsApp Group mein post karein, designated Regional Sales Owner ko tag karein, aur free site audit ya Experience Centre visit offer karein.

**C7 – Routed to Partner (B2F):** Site IndiFrame ki direct service coverage ke bahar hai. Authorized Green Fortune partner route offer karein aur CRM accordingly update karein.

**Pricing rule:** Sirf “Starts from ₹450 per sq ft onwards” plus applicable GST quote karein. Initial qualification ke dauran customized price calculate na karein.

**Authorized warranty claims:** 15-year profile warranty, 5-year hardware warranty aur 7-year colour warranty.
