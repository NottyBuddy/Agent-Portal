## 7. Window & Door Styles — Quick Guide
- **Sliding Windows/Doors:** Shutters tracks par sideways slide karte hain. Modern homes aur balconies mein commonly use hote hain.
- **Casement Windows/Doors:** Hinges par inward ya outward open hote hain. Sealing aur performance exact system/configuration par depend karti hai.
- **Fixed Windows:** Non-opening glass panels; natural light aur outside view ke liye.
- **Tilt & Turn:** Dual-action system; top se tilt ventilation ya poora swing-open mode. Exact configuration verify karein.
- **Sliding Folding / Slide & Fold:** Panels fold hokar opening ko wide access dete hain. Panel count/configuration catalogue aur technical approval ke hisaab se confirm karein.
- **Bay & Combination:** Projecting multi-section windows ya multiple panels ke modular combinations.
- **Lift & Slide Doors:** Large openings ke liye engineered sliding mechanism; size/suitability technical team confirm karegi.

## 11. Six Mandatory Discovery Questions — English Source Fields
1. Project Type: “Is this project for your personal residential home or a commercial property?” Capture Residential / Commercial / Villa / Apartment.
2. Site Location: “Which specific city and area/locality is your construction site located in?” Capture area + PIN code; verify serviceability.
3. Stakeholder Role: “Are you managing the build directly, or working with an architect or interior designer?” Capture Self / Architect / Civil Contractor / Designer.
4. Construction Stage: “What stage is the site currently in—Foundation, Brickwork, Plastering, or Finishing?” Capture Foundation / Brickwork / Plastering / Interiors.
5. Quantity Estimate: “Approximately how many window and door apertures/openings do you need to cover?” Capture estimated window/door count.
6. Aperture Readiness: “Has internal and external plastering been completed around all window openings?” Capture Yes (ready) / No (in progress).

## 15. Construction Stages — Agent Guidance
| Stage | Site activity | Window readiness | Agent action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Foundation / Footing | Groundwork started | Very early, usually 6+ months away | Capture basics; set future follow-up |
| Slab & Structure | Columns/RCC slabs underway | Around 4–6 months away | Capture details; follow construction timeline |
| Brickwork / Masonry | Walls/openings being built | Around 2–4 months away | Qualify fully; quantity estimate; firm follow-up |
| Plastering | Internal/external walls and openings being finished | Window planning stage | Qualify; hand over to Sales; offer free consultation and professional measurement |
| Granite / Marble Sill | Sills being installed | Important pre-installation stage | Confirm sill work completed before scheduling installation |
| Flooring / Electrical / Plumbing | Internal finishing underway | Often ready/urgent | Priority handover; flag urgency to Sales |
| Painting & Interiors | Final finishing | Ready and possibly late | Urgent handover; Sales to coordinate timing |

**Customer statements ka meaning**
- “Just started / Foundation / Slab work chal raha hai”: Months away. Stage note karein, requirement capture karein, future follow-up set karein.
- “Brickwork chal raha hai”: Window stage ke kareeb. Quantity/openings qualify karein aur firm follow-up date set karein.
- “Plastering chal rahi hai / complete hai”: Site window planning ke liye ready ho sakti hai. Sales ko handover karein; free consultation/measurement offer karein.
- “Granite/marble work chal raha hai”: Sill work complete hone ke baad installation plan karein.
- “Granite/marble work complete hai”: Openings installation readiness ke kareeb. Sales ko measurement, quotation aur installation planning ke liye handover karein.
- “Flooring/electrical/plumbing chal raha hai”: Priority handover; urgency flag karein.
- “Painting/interiors chal rahe hain”: Urgent handover; Sales ko timing coordinate karne ke liye immediately flag karein.
