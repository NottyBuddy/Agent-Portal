FINAL SCENARIO-WISE FULL CALL SCRIPT
Customer Qualification • Follow-Up • Objection Handling • Closing & Handover
1. Agent Conversation Principles
 Treat every call like a helpful consultation, not a rigid questionnaire.
 Ask one question at a time and allow the customer to respond before continuing.
 Listen actively, acknowledge concerns, and keep the tone supportive throughout the 
conversation.
 Keep explanations simple and avoid unnecessary technical terminology.
 Do not criticize competitors or local fabricators; redirect the discussion toward IndiFrame's 
value.
 Do not promise unauthorized discounts, exact delivery dates, structural approvals, wind-load 
calculations, mm-level specifications, chamber counts, or other technical details that require 
technical validation.
 Every connected call must end with a clear, agreed next action.
 Before ending, reconfirm the customer's requirement and the agreed action, team, date, and 
time wherever applicable.
 Installation is best planned after granite or marble sill work is completed, helping ensure proper 
fit and reduce the risk of site damage.
2. Mandatory Discovery Questions
These six questions should be completed before assigning the lead stage, wherever the conversation 
allows.
Project Type: Is this project for your personal residential home or commercial property?
Site Location: Which specific city and area/locality is your construction site located in?
Stakeholder Role: Are you managing the build directly, or working with an architect or interior 
designer?
Construction Stage: What stage is the site currently in — Foundation, Brickwork, Plastering, or 
Finishing?
Quantity Estimate: Approximately how many window and door apertures/openings do you need to 
cover?
Aperture Readiness: Have internal and external plastering been completed around all window 
openings?

3. Lead Stage & Routing Rules
C0 – New Unqualified Lead: Initial lead ingestion. Initiate the first call immediately upon assignment.
C1 – Future Potential Lead: Foundation, slab, brickwork, or plastering is incomplete. Schedule 
follow-up based on the projected readiness date.
C2 – Active / Qualified Lead: Plastering is complete and apertures are ready for audit. Post contact 
details in the Daily Leads WhatsApp Group, tag the designated Regional Sales Owner, and offer a 
free site audit or showroom/Experience Centre visit.
C7 – Routed to Partner (B2F): Site is outside direct IndiFrame service coverage. Offer the authorized 
Green Fortune partner route and update CRM accordingly.
Pricing rule: quote only “Starts from ₹450 per sq ft onwards” plus applicable GST. Do not calculate 
custom prices during initial qualification.
Authorized warranty claims: 15-year profile warranty, 5-year hardware warranty, and 7-year color 
warranty.
