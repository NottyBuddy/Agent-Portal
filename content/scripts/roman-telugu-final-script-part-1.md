## 1. Agent Conversation Principles

- Prathi call ni oka helpful consultation la handle cheyyandi; fixed questionnaire la undakoodadu.
- Oka sari oka question maatrame adagandi. Next question adige mundu customer ki answer cheyyadaniki time ivvandi.
- Customer cheppedi active ga vinandi, vaari concerns ni acknowledge cheyyandi, conversation antha supportive tone maintain cheyyandi.
- Explanations simple ga unchandi. Avasyaram leni technical terms vadakandi.
- Competitors leda local fabricators ni criticize cheyyakandi; discussion ni IndiFrame value vaipu redirect cheyyandi.
- Unauthorized discounts, exact delivery dates, structural approvals, wind-load calculations, mm-level specifications, chamber counts, leda technical validation avasaramaina vere details ni promise cheyyakandi.
- Prathi connected call clear ga mutually agreed next action tho end avvali.
- Call end cheyyadaniki mundu, applicable ayithe customer requirement mariyu agreed action, team, date, time ni malli confirm cheyyandi.
- Granite leda marble sill work complete ayina taruvatha installation plan cheyyadam better. Dini valla proper fit ki help avutundi, site damage risk taggutundi.

## 2. Mandatory Discovery Questions

Conversation allow chesina prathi situation lo lead stage assign cheyyadaniki mundu ee six questions complete cheyyandi.

**Project Type:** Ee project mee personal residential home kosama, leka commercial property kosama?

**Site Location:** Mee construction site exact ga ye city mariyu area/locality lo undi?

**Stakeholder Role:** Construction ni meeru direct ga manage chestunnara, leka architect leda interior designer kuda involved unnara?

**Construction Stage:** Present site ye stage lo undi — Foundation, Brickwork, Plastering, leka Finishing?

**Quantity Estimate:** Approximately enni window mariyu door apertures/openings cover cheyyali?

**Aperture Readiness:** Anni window openings chuttu internal mariyu external plastering complete ayyinda?

## 3. Lead Stage & Routing Rules

**C0 – New Unqualified Lead:** Lead system lo first time vachindi. Assignment vachina ventane first call initiate cheyyandi.

**C1 – Future Potential Lead:** Foundation, slab, brickwork, leka plastering inka complete kaaledu. Expected readiness date prakaram follow-up schedule cheyyandi.

**C2 – Active / Qualified Lead:** Plastering complete ayyindi, apertures audit ki ready ga unnayi. Customer contact details ni Daily Leads WhatsApp Group lo post cheyyandi, designated Regional Sales Owner ni tag cheyyandi, free site audit leda Experience Centre visit offer cheyyandi.

**C7 – Routed to Partner (B2F):** Site IndiFrame direct service coverage bayata undi. Authorized Green Fortune partner route offer chesi CRM ni accordingly update cheyyandi.

**Pricing rule:** “Starts from ₹450 per sq ft onwards” plus applicable GST maatrame quote cheyyandi. Initial qualification time lo customized price calculate cheyyakandi.

**Authorized warranty claims:** 15-year profile warranty, 5-year hardware warranty, mariyu 7-year colour warranty.
