Integrated Supply Chain & Quality Control

Complete Responsibility. Zero Gaps
IndiFrame takes full ownership of every step: from proﬁle manufacturing to ﬁnal
installation. This eliminates fragmentation and reduces the common risks associated
with multi-vendor models, and helps manage the supply chain end-to-end, giving
customers one point of responsibility for quality, timelines, and service

Quality Assurance & Standards
IndiFrame follows a rigorous 30+ checkpoint quality process before any order is
dispatched. Our proﬁles and ﬁnished systems are tested at government-certiﬁed
CIPET laboratories and conform to:

                                                                  5 YEARS

BIS Standards                EN ISO and ASTM            RERA-aligned 5-year warranty
                           international standards        for manufacturing defects

Each apartment or home receives an individual warranty card, enabling end
customers to contact IndiFrame directly, reducing post-handover burden on
developers.

Digital Control from Estimates to Installation
Through our integrated Twin-Tech system (OpsGate + PartnerGate), every project
milestone estimation, surveys, design approval, production, dispatch, installation,
and service is tracked on a single dashboard. This visibility empowers developers,
architects, and homeowners with clarity and predictable execution.
